DLR cycle counters

How many people cycle past each of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown County Council’s permanent bike counters, updated when the council publishes new data.

Part of Cycling infrastructure in Dublin. The Seapoint and People’s Park counters are on the Coastal Mobility Route, and the Rock Road counters are just south of the gap at Merrion Gates. The Blackrock DART laneway page uses both.

Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown County Council (DLR) has permanent automatic counters on its main cycle routes. They count every bike that passes, every hour, and the council publishes the counts as open data. This page turns them into charts. It checks for new data every week and updates itself when there is some.

Data to 30 September 2026, from 16 counters. Counting started in 2010.

About the data

  • What’s counted. Most counters count bikes only. Where a counter sits on a shared path, DLR publishes the bike count separately. One-way counters only see one direction, so they show roughly half the traffic of a two-way counter on the same road.
  • Faults. Counters break, lose power or get their batteries changed, which shows up as days of zero or near zero. Days with fewer than 22 hours of data, a total of zero, or a total under a quarter of that counter’s median over the surrounding four weeks are left out. Months with fewer than 10 good days are left blank.
  • Updates. DLR updates the dataset roughly monthly, and sometimes publishes a year’s counts in one go. A weekly check downloads any new files and redraws this page.
  • Code and data. fetch_cycle_counts.py downloads the counts. make_counter_page.py draws this page. The monthly averages and summary are in analysis/cycling/data as CSV.
