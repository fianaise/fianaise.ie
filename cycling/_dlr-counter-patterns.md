**Days of the week.** On a typical counter the busiest weekdays are Wednesday and Tuesday. Compared with the weekday average, Tuesday is 9% above, Thursday is 2% below and Friday is 17% below.

Weekends are quieter at most counters, most of all on commuter routes: N11 Stillorgan Road, Montrose (-63%) and N11 before UCD (-60%) compared with weekdays. The exception is People's Park (+21%), which is busier at weekends.

**Time of year.** All 12 counters are quietest in December or January. Routes along the coast and through parks are busiest in July or August: Seapoint (Coastal Mobility Route) (Jul), People's Park (Jul), York Road (Aug), Rock Road park path (Jul), Wyattville Road, park gates (Aug). The commuter routes peak in September, when college and school are back: Clonskeagh Road (Sep), Rock Road bus lane (Sep), N11 before UCD (Sep), N11 Stillorgan Road, inbound (Sep). On a typical counter the busiest month has about 2.2 times as many cyclists a day as the quietest.
