What it assumes

  • Income tax uses the 2027 rates, bands and credits from Budget 2027, kept the same for every year, with no inflation or pay rises. Incomes are treated as employment income. A married couple pays the lower of joint assessment and being taxed separately. USC and PRSI are left out because they are charged on each person separately either way.
  • Left out, so the real gap can be bigger: the Home Carer Tax Credit (up to €2,050 a year), which only married couples and civil partners can claim.
  • Inheritance tax assumes the partner who inherits has had no earlier gifts or inheritances that count towards the same €22,000 threshold, and that no relief applies. The dwelling house exemption can cover the family home, if strict conditions are met, and a court order under the Cohabitants Act 2010 is tax free.
  • Capital gains tax is the tax on a gift between partners that a spouse wouldn’t pay. It uses the 31% rate and the €1,270 annual exemption.

The rules are in tax-rules.js, with a source for each number, and are updated after each Budget.
